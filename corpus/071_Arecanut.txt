CROP INFORMATION

Crop_ID: C071
Crop_Name: Arecanut
Scientific_Name: Areca catechu
Crop_Type: Plantation
Suitable_Season: Perennial
Preferred_Soil: Deep well-drained loam
Water_Requirement: High
Growth_Duration: 5-7 years to bearing
Rainfall_or_Irrigation: 1500-4000 mm

CULTIVATION
Planting_Method: Healthy seedlings
Cultivation_Practice: Use healthy planting material, prepare a suitable seedbed, maintain suitable spacing, remove weeds early, and monitor the crop regularly.
Irrigation: Provide water according to crop stage and soil moisture; avoid prolonged waterlogging unless the crop specifically tolerates it.
Nutrient_Management: Apply organic manure and balanced NPK based on soil-test results and local agricultural recommendations.
Weed_Management: Timely hand weeding/intercultivation and locally recommended herbicide options where appropriate.

PESTS AND DISEASES
Major_Diseases: Koleroga fruit rot; yellow leaf disease; bud rot
Common_Pests: Spindle bug; mite; root grub
Disease_Prevention: Maintain shade, drainage and balanced nutrition
Pest_Management: Scout the crop regularly, conserve beneficial organisms, remove heavily affected plant material, and use integrated pest management.
Pesticide_Guidance: Use locally registered, label-approved products only; verify with an agricultural expert

SEED_INFORMATION
Seed_or_Planting_Material: Use certified, healthy, disease-free seed, seedlings, cuttings, setts, rhizomes, suckers, or grafted plants as appropriate for the crop.
Seed_Treatment: Follow the seed-treatment recommendation on the product label or from a local agricultural expert before planting.

HARVESTING_AND_STORAGE
Harvesting: Harvest mature bunches
Storage: Clean, grade and dry harvested produce appropriately; store in a clean, dry, ventilated facility suitable for the commodity.
Post_Harvest_Problems: Moisture damage, fungal spoilage, insects, bruising or quality loss can occur if handling and storage conditions are poor.

MAJOR_USES: Nut crop
SAFETY_NOTE: Pesticide choices and application rates vary by crop, pest, location and current registration. Use only legally registered products and follow the label and local agricultural guidance.
